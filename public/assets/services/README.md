# Servicio de Regalo

All photographs and presentation pages come from the user-provided `catalogo/SERVICIO OBSEQUIO BABY CITY (2).pdf` (3 pages).

- `gift-boxes.webp`: photograph from page 1.
- `gift-details.webp`: photograph from page 2.
- `gift-example-01.webp` through `gift-example-09.webp`: all nine original photographs from page 3.
- `presentation-1.webp` through `presentation-3.webp`: complete rendered pages, including their original artwork and text.

Embedded photos were extracted with PyMuPDF and resized/encoded as WebP using Pillow. No AI-generated images were used. The original PDF remains available for download. The published S/ 6 price and included wrapping details are transcribed from page 2.
